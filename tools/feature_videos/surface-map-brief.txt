Surface Map is an existing public tool at /surface-map, linked from the homepage.
It requires no active rider and can use local cached world and route data.
The sidebar contains a World selector, route search, All/Cycling/Running filters,
and Race/Free Ride route version buttons. Not all routes have both versions.
The world map colors roads by surface type and includes a surface legend.
Selecting a route shows its highlighted path, distance and climbing statistics,
surface breakdown, and a surface-colored elevation profile.
Copy link shares the selected world, route, and route mode in the URL.
The example demonstrates Watopia and Road to Ruins in the default Race mode.
