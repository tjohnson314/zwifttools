Looking up current profile FTP...

Use the FTP you used for this ride, which may differ from your current FTP. Fixed-reference FTP tests use the reference from their plan instead, and disable this field. Positive start offsets move the plan later; negative offsets allow a workout that started before recording. Intensity applies to the whole plan. Pauses, skipped blocks, and mid-ride intensity changes are not automatically detected.

Distance-based dynamic workouts are not included in this time-based comparison.

Power comparison

Actual power is raw, including zero and unusual readings. Shading shows prescribed target ranges; free-ride and unresolved targets have no planned line. Interval means are time-weighted over recorded samples only. Samples extend to the next timestamp, at most one typical recording interval; gaps and missing power are excluded. Planned targets are rounded to the nearest 5 W after FTP and intensity scaling, including ramp steps. Target averages and errors use those same covered times. Range errors use the target midpoint; averages across steps or ranges may fall between 5 W increments.
